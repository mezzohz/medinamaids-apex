Government-mandated costs

Separate from our fee — paid by every employer.

We’re showing these so you see the complete picture.

  • Maid Levy

    S$60/mo

    Concessionary rate for most families (young children, elderly, or persons with disabilities). Standard rate is S$300/mo.

  • Helper Salary

    S$580 – S$750/mo

    Depends on nationality & experience. Paid monthly to your helper.

Industry comparison: most Singapore agencies charge S$2,500 – S$4,000+ for the same scope, with the security bond and basic medical billed separately — premium upgrades cost an additional S$400–800.
